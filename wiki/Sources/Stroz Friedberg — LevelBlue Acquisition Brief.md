# Stroz Friedberg — LevelBlue Acquisition Brief

> Short briefing on Stroz Friedberg — DFIR + cybercrime-response firm acquired by [[LevelBlue]] in August 2025 via the Aon Cybersecurity & IP Litigation deal.

## Summary

- Stroz Friedberg Inc., founded 2000, is a global DFIR / digital-risk firm — digital forensics, cybercrime + data-breach response, eDiscovery, business intelligence, investigations.
- Client base: corporations, law firms, governments, individuals — typically engaged during crisis (IP, financial, reputational threats).
- Leadership pedigree: former FBI cybercrime leaders, U.S. cybercrime prosecutors, senior government / law-enforcement / cybersecurity professionals.
- Pre-Level Blue ownership: backed by [[New Mountain]] after a multi-year strategic review of related sectors — investment was negotiated bilaterally outside a competitive auction, leveraging New Mountain's relationships with the firm's management.
- 2025-08 — acquired by [[LevelBlue]] as part of the Aon Cybersecurity & IP Litigation purchase (alongside Elysium Digital).

## Why we care

Stroz Friedberg becomes a sibling DFIR / incident-response unit inside [[LevelBlue]]. Potential collaboration surface for [[IRCA]] / [[IRCA Cloud]] / [[Incident Investigation (for Fusion 2)]] and any future Level-Blue-wide IR tooling. Also expands the Level Blue acquisition stack the DS team must reason about when modelling customer / org / data boundaries.
