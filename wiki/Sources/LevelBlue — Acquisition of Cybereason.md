# LevelBlue — Acquisition of Cybereason

## Summary

LevelBlue (a 2024 spinout from AT&T's cybersecurity division, majority-owned by WillJam Ventures) acquired Cybereason in late 2025. Closing brought SoftBank Corp., SoftBank Vision Fund 2, and Liberty Strategic Capital in as strategic investors, and added Steven T. Mnuchin to LevelBlue's board. This is the active org context for all DS work in this vault.

## Key dates

- **Oct 14 2025** — acquisition signed.
- **Nov 25 2025** — acquisition closed.

## What LevelBlue is

- World's largest pure-play managed security services provider (MSSP).
- HQ Dallas, Texas. CEO / Chairman: Robert "Bob" McCullen.
- Inherited from AT&T: managed services, consulting, Open Threat Exchange (OTX), AlienVault assets.
- 2025 acquisitions consolidated the field: **Alert Logic** (Fortra), **Aon Cybersecurity & IP Litigation** (incl. Stroz Friedberg, Elysium Digital), **Trustwave** (incl. SpiderLabs), and **Cybereason**.
- Strategic partnerships: SentinelOne, Fortra, AT&T.

## What Cybereason brings

- XDR platform, MDR services, DFIR expertise.
- Active product / R&D portfolio — see `raw/projects/` for the DS slice.
