# Jose Manuel Martin Rodriguez

> Lead Cybersecurity in Labs.

## Background

Jose Manuel Martin Rodriguez is a Lead Cybersecurity in Labs, based in Spain, reporting to [[Santiago Cortes Diaz]]. Five-year Telecommunications Engineering degree (Spain) + MSc Cybersecurity; started his career in software development at Telefonica. Ten years with the company through three rebrands — [[AlienVault]] → [[AT&T]] → [[LevelBlue]] — authoring correlation rules and [[Suricata Signatures]] for the [[USMA]] (Unified Security Management) platform. Recently positioned as a domain expert who builds datasets and translates cybersecurity concepts for pure data scientists lacking a security background. Previously implemented the original unsupervised-ML UBA component inside USMA (anomalous logins, exfiltrations, lateral movements) — the seed experience behind the [[Rebuild UEBA in Phoenix XDR]] idea.
