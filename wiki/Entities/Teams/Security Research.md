# Security Research

> Cybereason / Level Blue threat detection content + research org-unit —
> rules, malware analysis, threat intel feeding the product stack.

## Mission

Produce detection rules, malware analysis, and threat-intel content;
research adversary tradecraft; collaborate with the
[[Data Science Team]] on ML-augmented detection ([[Owlint-Sigma]], [[OWLINT]],
[[Rules Quality]], [[Detection Engineering Hub]]). Includes the
Trustwave-inherited research staff that now sits inside [[Spider Labs]]
under [[LevelBlue]] but remains catalogued here for org continuity.
