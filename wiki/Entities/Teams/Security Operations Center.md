# Security Operations Center

> Cybereason's MDR analyst ops + threat-intel triage unit — the human
> analysts on the receiving end of platform alerts.

## Mission

Run managed detection-and-response (MDR) analyst operations, intake
threat intelligence, and act as primary downstream consumer of the
[[Cybereason]] platform's correlation + detection output. Provides
human ground truth + feedback for DS-team projects ([[Hunter]],
[[IRCA]], [[AI Assistant]]) and is the buyer of analyst-facing GenAI
features.
