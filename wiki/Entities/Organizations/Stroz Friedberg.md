# Stroz Friedberg

> Global digital-risk / DFIR firm — digital forensics, cybercrime and data-breach response, eDiscovery, business intelligence, investigations. Acquired by [[LevelBlue]] in August 2025 via the Aon Cybersecurity & IP Litigation deal.

## What they do

Digital risk management for corporations, law firms, governments, and individuals — typically engaged in crisis: IP theft, financial fraud, reputational threats, data breaches. Service lines: digital forensics, incident response, cybercrime investigation, eDiscovery, business intelligence, investigative due diligence.

Founded 2000. Senior staff drawn from former FBI cybercrime leadership, U.S. cybercrime prosecutors, and senior government / law-enforcement / cybersecurity backgrounds.

## Relationship to DS team

Sibling unit under [[LevelBlue]] post-2025-08 acquisition. - 2025-08 — Acquired by [[LevelBlue]] as part of the Aon Cybersecurity & IP Litigation acquisition (alongside Elysium Digital).
