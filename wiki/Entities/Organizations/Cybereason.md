# Cybereason

> XDR / EDR / MDR cybersecurity vendor acquired by [[LevelBlue]] on
> 2025-11-25; the employer of record for the Data Science team.

## What they do

Endpoint and extended detection-and-response (EDR / XDR) platform plus
managed detection-and-response (MDR) services. Core product line: the
Cybereason platform (sensor agent → cloud-side correlation → analyst
console). Active R&D portfolio in LLM-for-security, malware analysis,
detection engineering. See `raw/projects/` for the DS-team slice.

## Relationship to DS team

Employer. The Data Science team sits inside [[Engineering]] at
Cybereason and partners with [[Security Research]], the [[Security Operations Center]], and [[Labs]]. Post-acquisition (2025-11-25), DS
roadmap alignment with [[LevelBlue]]'s MSSP product strategy is in
scope — see [[LevelBlue — Acquisition of Cybereason]].
