# Recorded Future

> Large commercial threat-intelligence vendor. Referenced as an external benchmark for [[Infrastructure of Interest]] lead-time claims.

## What they do

Threat-intel-as-a-service — feeds, dashboards, and analyst workflows covering adversaries, infrastructure, vulnerabilities, and brand exposure. Major incumbent in the commercial CTI market.

## Relationship to DS team

External attribution benchmark for IOI lead-time validation. Documented case (2026-05-14 brownbag): a domain present in IOI from August 2025 was independently flagged by Recorded Future in February 2026 — a **177-day** IOI lead time over a top-tier commercial intel vendor.
