# AlienVault

> Original vendor of the [[USMA]] Unified Security Management platform; acquired by [[AT&T]] in 2018 and folded into [[LevelBlue]] via the May 2024 cybersecurity-arm spinout.

## What they do

Historical — AlienVault built the original Unified Security Management (USM) platform (correlation engine + IDS) and the [Open Threat Exchange](https://otx.alienvault.com/) (OTX) crowd-sourced threat-intel feed. After the AT&T acquisition the AlienVault product lines (USM Anywhere / [[USMA]], OTX) became the core of AT&T Cybersecurity, which was then spun out into [[LevelBlue]] in May 2024.

## Relationship to DS team

Indirect / historical. AlienVault no longer exists as a standalone entity — the DS team's relationship is via the products it bequeathed (USMA, OTX) which are now operated by [[LevelBlue]]. Several long-tenured Level Blue engineers — including [[Jose Manuel Martin Rodriguez]] — started their careers here ten years ago and have continued through the AT&T and Level Blue rebrands.
