# Hunt.io

> Third-party threat-intelligence vendor (attack-surface + adversary-infra hunting). Referenced as an external benchmark for [[Infrastructure of Interest]] lead-time claims.

## What they do

Adversary-infrastructure intelligence — scans and catalogs internet-facing infrastructure (C2 panels, phishing kits, lookalike domains) and exposes verdicts to customers.

## Relationship to DS team

External attribution benchmark, not a vendor relationship. Documented case (2026-05-14 brownbag): a domain flagged by [[Infrastructure of Interest]] in September 2025 was independently classified malicious by Hunt.io in April 2026 — used to validate IOI lead-time claims (~7 months ahead in this instance).

- Could Hunt.io become an IOI collector source (vs. just an attribution benchmark)?
