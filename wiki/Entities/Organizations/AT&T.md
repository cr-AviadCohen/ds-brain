# AT&T

> US telecom giant; historical parent of [[LevelBlue]]'s MSSP business
> via the May 2024 AT&T-cybersecurity / WillJam Ventures spinout.

## What they do

Telecommunications carrier (wireless, broadband, fiber) and historically
operator of one of the world's largest managed-security-services
practices. AT&T's cybersecurity arm was built around the [[AlienVault]]
acquisition (2018) — AlienVault's USM (Unified Security Management)
platform and Open Threat Exchange (OTX) became the core of AT&T
Cybersecurity. In May 2024 AT&T spun out the whole cybersecurity unit
(managed services, consulting, OTX, AlienVault assets) into a joint
venture with WillJam Ventures — that joint venture became [[LevelBlue]],
and [[USMA]] (USM Anywhere) is the MSSP-platform inheritance line that
runs AlienVault → AT&T → Level Blue.

## Relationship to DS team

Indirect / historical. AT&T does not own the DS team — the DS team's
current parent is [[LevelBlue]] (via [[Cybereason]]). AT&T's relevance
is as the source of [[LevelBlue]]'s MSSP DNA (managed services, OTX,
AlienVault product lines) and as a continuing strategic partner.
