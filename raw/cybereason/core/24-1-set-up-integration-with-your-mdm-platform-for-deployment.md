In order to add devices and to manage device/user information,
you use and maintain a Unified Endpoint Manager (UEM) or
Mobile Device Management (MDM) platform. The UEM/MDM
platform contains all device and user information and enables a
streamlined point of deployment across your organization.
Your Mobile Threat Defense instance integrates with a number of
UEM/MDM platforms. After you create the relevant activation
profiles for your devices, you add the activation profile
configuration during the UEM/MDM configuration process.
For details on how to perform the steps necessary to connect the
platform, see the following pages in the Jamf Security
documentation:
