Cybereason supports single sign-on (SSO) authentication for all
users. Single sign-on authentication gives administrators better
control over password management by allowing them to integrate
their company's current authentication services with the
Cybereason platform.
Benefits to using SSO authentication include:
Users do not need to remember additional passwords
Administrators do not need to change access permissions in
multiple places if an employee leaves the company
Users can conveniently pivot between company applications
and the Cybereason console
