If an administrator or other member of your organization discovers


the real identity of an anonymous user, you have the ability to re
do the anonymization process. This will change the association of


anonymous identities to their devices. As a result, performing the
anonymization process again may cause difficulty for analysts and


admins since the association between anonymous user names

and devices is now different and the platform may not be able to


connect the new anonymous identity and device to the previous

device for the identity.


In some cases, there are network-based services and applications

that are not usable with the default settings used by Cybereason


Mobile. In these cases, you can add network exceptions to help

your device users connect to the services and applications they


need.


Your Cybereason Mobile team will help you add these exceptions


in your Cybereason Mobile environment and define how existing

Cybereason Mobile protection will apply to these services and


applications.
