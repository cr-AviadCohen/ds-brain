This section is relevant for customers that are not integrating
mobile device data with all other Cybereason data and that
manage their devices separately from the Cybereason
platform, using the RADAR console and their own MDM/UEM
instance.
Mobile Threat Defense provides you with a way to manage
protection of your mobile devices.
Using the RADAR console, you can add devices and manage a
variety of settings for your mobile devices, including:
Add and monitor devices
Deploy devices
Update the security protection for your devices to ensure an
appropriate level of protection and response
You can also set up a number of optional settings around data
usage, network traffic, and other settings.
The topics in this section enable you to use your RADAR console.
