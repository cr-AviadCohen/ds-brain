The sensor grouping for local analysts feature requires an
upgrade of the Data Platform infrastructure. To understand
when this feature will be available to you, please contact your
Customer Success representative.
The Local analyst L1, L2, and L3 roles allows system
administrators to delegate investigation and MalOp management
tasks to different users or teams, while limiting the access each
team has to other data. This allows users with the Local analyst
L1, L2, or L3 role to better access and act on data relevant to the
sensors in their assigned sensor group.
If you are an MSSP for various different organizations, you can use
the Local Analyst role to invite their customers to see only the
MalOps for their groups, while still using a single Cybereason
console to manage all your organizations at the same time.
Note
If you are an MSSP using the Local Analyst role for customers,
Cybereason recommends you use SSO or TFA for your
customer users.
