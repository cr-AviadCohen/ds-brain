To help you better understand and classify all MalOps generated
in your environment, the Cybereason platform further classifies
MalOps based on their activity and detection types.
This section describes MalOp detection types and root causes.
This section also provides recommended first steps for
responding to each MalOp.
Note
The Cybereason platform also detects behaviors that do not
generate MalOps but instead generate Evidence and
Suspicion. The specifics of these behaviors are beyond the
scope of this section. For more information, contact Customer
Success.
