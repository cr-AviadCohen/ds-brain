Malicious process behaviors include actions by processes
classified or detected as malicious or processes running in
strange ways. The MalOp types listed in this topic are triggered by
various malicious process behaviors.
These MalOps are part of the RESEARCH stability group.

The 'Malicious creation of a child process by Microsoft Office
process' MalOp is triggered when a MS Office process launches a
child process that performs malicious activities.
Supported OS for this MalOp: macOS
