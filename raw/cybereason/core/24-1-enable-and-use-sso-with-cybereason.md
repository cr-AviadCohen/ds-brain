Enable SSO
To use SSO authentication in your environment, first contact your
Cybereason Customer Success representative. They will enable
the feature for your environment.
After SSO is enabled for your environment, user administrators
can enable SSO for a specific user from the Users screen when
editing a particular user.
Note
If SSO is enabled for your account, you cannot use the
Cybereason API. If you need to use the Cybereason API, you
must create a dedicated Cybereason user without SSO
enabled.
