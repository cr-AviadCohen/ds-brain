If you need to remove the Cybereason Mobile sensor from your
devices, you must follow specific instructions for each type of
device. Read the relevant section for instructions on how to
remove devices for iOS or Android devices.
Note
If your device was added to Cybereason Mobile through an
MDM platform, you do not need to perform these steps. Your
Cybereason Mobile administrator performs the necessary
removal and the sensor is silently removed from your device.

Android devices
iOS devices
Before you remove the Cybereason Mobile sensor from a device,
your Cybereason Mobile administrator should record the Device
Removal code for the device. You will need this information to
finish the device removal process.
On your device, do the following:
1. Navigate to the screen with the Cybereason Mobile app and
press and hold the app until the X symbol displays.
2. Press the X symbol to delete the app.
3. Open the Settings app.
4. In the Settings app, open the General > Profile screen.
5. Select Wandera Cellular Profile.
6. In the Profile information, click Delete Profile.
7. Enter your device passcode.
8. If needed, when prompted, enter the device removal code.
9. Turn off the device Wifi.
