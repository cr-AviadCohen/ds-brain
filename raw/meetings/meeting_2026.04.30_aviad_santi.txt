## Meeting Summary
* Meeting Objective & Work Philosophy The meeting served as an introduction between Aviad (AI Architect) and Santi (Director Cybersecurity) to explore how machine learning and generative AI can be integrated into threat intelligence and security research. Santi emphasized a specific philosophy regarding Product Management (PM): PMs should define the business problems rather than dictate the technical solutions. Using metaphors—like installing mirrors in slow elevators to distract people, or the Russians using a simple pencil in space while the Americans spent millions inventing an anti-gravity pen—they agreed that simple, creative solutions are often superior to complex engineering. Santi also strongly advocated for dedicating a specific "bandwidth" for engineers to improve internal capabilities, completely independent of PM-driven product deadlines.
* Santi’s Core Projects Santi detailed three primary initiatives his team (alongside his main data science analyst, Jose) is currently advancing:
    * The Sigma Pipeline: A unified system that extracts attacker behaviors (TTPs) from threat reports and blogs, and translates them into generic Sigma rules. These universal rules can then be seamlessly converted and deployed across various SIEM solutions (like Phoenix).
    * Tipper: An LLM-powered knowledge database and chatbot (utilizing Bedrock). It automatically ingests public threat reports and extracts actionable data, such as malicious IPs, vulnerabilities, and ransomware tags.
    * Infrastructure of Interest: An ML-based project that monitors suspicious domains by evaluating roughly 60 different features (e.g., domain age, antivirus alarms). A critical aspect of this model is tracking "behavioral exposure"—measuring how many unique customers and assets interact with a domain over rolling 6-hour windows. By analyzing this exposure dynamically, the team can assess risk levels and identify exponential, rapid spreading. This specific anomaly is a prime indicator of supply chain attacks, where legitimate software (like Firefox) is compromised and spreads malware during routine updates.
* Personal & Spiritual Connection A significant portion of the meeting shifted into a deep, personal conversation regarding geopolitics and spirituality. Both men expressed profound sorrow over the ongoing conflicts in Israel and Iran, as well as the historical trauma of the Spanish Civil War, agreeing that war is futile and "suffering never ends". They strongly bonded over shared spiritual practices, including yoga and meditation. Aviad shared his belief in the "Law of One" (choosing "service to others") and discussed his recent mind-opening experiences with psychedelics. Unconventionally, Aviad even proposed introducing guided meditations at work as a way to solve complex technical problems by tapping into the subconscious mind.
* Next Steps The meeting concluded with a plan to schedule a broader collaborative session next week involving Inbar's entire team and Jose. The agenda will consist of a 20-minute high-level overview of the projects, followed by a 20-minute practical deep dive into the data behind the 'Infrastructure of Interest' project.
