# LevelBlue — Products & Services

LevelBlue's portfolio spans **managed security services**, **consulting & professional services**, **security software**, and **security operations platforms**. Many capabilities were strengthened through 2025 acquisitions (Trustwave, Cybereason, Stroz Friedberg, Elysium Digital, Fortra's Alert Logic).

---

| Product / Service | Description |
|---|---|
| **Threat Detection, Investigation and Response (TDIR)** | Identify, investigate, and eliminate threats with AI-driven precision and expert insight. |
