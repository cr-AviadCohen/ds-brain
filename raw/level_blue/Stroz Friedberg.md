LevelBlue acquired 'Stroz Friedberg' company on August 2025.

Stroz Friedberg Inc., founded in 2000, is a global firm specializing in digital risk management, digital forensics, cybercrime and data breach response, eDiscovery, business intelligence, and investigations.

The firm supports corporations, law firms, governments, and individuals in managing digital-era risks, often during crisis situations involving intellectual property, financial, or reputational threats.

Its leadership and senior experts come from high-level legal, law enforcement, government, and cybersecurity backgrounds, including former FBI cybercrime leaders, U.S. cybercrime prosecutors, and senior government professionals.

New Mountain invested in Stroz Friedberg after a multi-year strategic review of related sectors, leveraging its industry relationships and reputation to work directly with the company’s management outside a competitive auction process.
