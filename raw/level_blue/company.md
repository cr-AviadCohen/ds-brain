- **Type:** Privately held joint venture (AT&T + WillJam Ventures, with later strategic investors)
- **Industry:** Cybersecurity / Managed Security Services Provider (MSSP)

- **Launched:** May 2024 (debuted at RSA Conference 2024)
- **Employees:** 1,000+ globally at launch; significantly expanded through 2025 acquisitions

## History & ownership

### Origin (2023–2024)
- **November 2023:** AT&T announced an agreement to spin out its cybersecurity division into a standalone managed cybersecurity services business, with capital investment from **WillJam Ventures** (a Chicago-based investor).
- **May 2024:** The new company officially launched as **LevelBlue** at RSA Conference 2024.
- **Inherited assets:** AT&T's managed services business, cybersecurity consulting business, and the **Open Threat Exchange (OTX)** community plus other assets from AT&T's 2018 acquisition of **AlienVault**.
- **Ownership at launch:** WillJam Ventures became the majority owner; AT&T retained a minority stake and board representation.
