# Security Engines descriptions

## AIAV

**AIAV Engine** is the next generation of antivirus technology. Powered by advanced AI and machine learning, it detects and prevents both known and unknown threats, keeping your endpoints safe and secure.

## Anti-ransomware

**Anti-Ransomware Engine** is our unique, behavior-based solution designed to detect and prevent ransomware attacks before it can cause harm, providing an extra layer of protection.

## Anti-stealer

Anti-Stealer is the industry's first solution specifically designed to detect and prevent stealer malwares, safeguarding your sensitive information from theft and ensuring your secrets are protected.

## App control

**Next-Gen App Control** reduces your organization's attack surface by proactively managing and securing applications, ensuring only trusted software runs on your systems.

## Behavioral execution prevention

Detect and block malicious processes with advanced **Behavioral Execution Prevention**, stopping threats before they can cause damage by analyzing and acting on suspicious behavior in real-time.

## CloudAV

**Cloud** **AIAV** Engine, powered by the cloud, provides an additional layer of advanced protection, enhancing security with near real-time threat detection, prevention, and cutting-edge big data threat intelligence.

## Exploit protection

**Exploit Protection** blocks attempts to exploit vulnerabilities on your organization's endpoints, preventing potential breaches and securing your systems from known application threats.

## Generic Shellcode Detection (GSD)

**Generic Shellcode Detection** blocks attempts to exploit memory-based vulnerabilities on your organization's endpoints using unique decoys, preventing malicious code from executing and safeguarding your systems from targeted attacks.

## Hash based execution block

**Hash-Based Execution Block** stops the execution of undesired processes on your organization's endpoints by identifying and blocking them based on their unique signature.

## Process integrity

**Process Integrity** automatically prevents any application from accessing or modifying the memory of other processes, ensuring that no app can inject code into higher-priority or protected processes, keeping your systems secure from unauthorized manipulation.

## Script protection

**Script Protection** detects and prevents script-based attacks, blocking malicious scripts before they can compromise your systems and data.

## Secret scanner

**Secret Scanner** is the industry's first solution designed to detect sensitive and risky information, such as API keys, passwords, and more, preventing unauthorized exposure within your organization.

## Variant payload prevention

**Variant Payload Prevention** detects and blocks the execution of malicious payloads across your organization, stopping advanced threats before they can cause damage.

# Agent settings

## Advanced agent features Advanced options provided by Cybereason to customize and allow preview features and special cases

## .

## Auto-decommissioning

Auto-Decommissioning automatically moves old endpoints to archive and releases their licenses.

## Auto-update

Auto-Update automatically upgrades the agent version based on your organization's policy, ensuring your systems are always running the latest security features without manual intervention.

## Device controls

**Device Control** allows you to set the control mode for managing removable devices on relevant endpoints, giving you full control over how Cybereason handles device access and ensuring data security.

## File events collection

File Events Collection enables the gathering of file events for specific file types crucial to your security needs, providing valuable insights to detect and respond to potential threats.

## Firewall controls

**Firewall Control** lets you configure host-based firewalls on endpoints, enforcing communication rules to block unauthorized access and enhance overall network security.

## Incident response tools

**Incident Response Tools** allow you to deploy and run response tools on endpoints, automatically uploading the results to a GCP bucket for streamlined analysis and quicker threat resolution.

## Proxy server

**Proxy Server** lets you configure proxy settings for the agent, ensuring seamless communication between the agents and the management server, even in network-restricted environments.

## Registry event collection

**Registry Event Collection** enables the collection of registry events across your environments, providing crucial insights for detecting and responding to potential security threats.

## Tampering protection

**Tampering Protection** prevents unauthorized tampering actions on agents, such as file removal or process termination, ensuring the integrity and effectiveness of your security defenses.

## User notifications **User Notifications** allows you to control the visibility of Cybereason notifications, giving you the option to show or hide alerts to end users based on your organization’s preferences.

## Visible on the system tray

**Visible on the System Tray** lets you show or hide the Cybereason icon in the system tray, providing control over its visibility to end users while maintaining security management.

# Custom rules and exclusions

## Exclusions

**Exclusions** lets you view and manage inherited exclusion lists, as well as add new exclusions, providing greater control over which files, processes, or paths are excluded from security engines and scans.
